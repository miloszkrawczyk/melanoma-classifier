ResNet18, pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned here
=================================================

First model in this project that was not built from scratch. Seventeen
convolutional layers arrive already trained on 1.28 million photos; only the
final classifier is new.

Setup
-----
model            ResNet18, 11,180,103 parameters
                 of which 11,176,512 come pretrained, 3,591 start random
head             Linear(512, 7) replacing ImageNet's Linear(512, 1000)
input            224x224 RGB
augmentation     same as the CNN: flips, RandomRotation(20), ColorJitter
normalisation    ImageNet statistics, because that is what its filters
                 were fed during pretraining
loss             CrossEntropyLoss with class weights (inverse frequency)
optimiser        Adam, lr 0.0001, ten times smaller than the CNN's
batch size       32
epochs           15
device           mps
split            by lesion_id, seed 42, 70/15/15
train/val/test   6986 / 1521 / 1508 images

What the numbers say
--------------------
Read as single figures, the ResNet looks like it barely moved the needle on
what this project cares about: 77.5% recall against the CNN's 77.8%. That
reading is wrong, and the threshold sweep shows why.

Both numbers come from the top-class rule, which is an arbitrary operating
point. What actually changed is the price of recall. To catch 95% of
malignant lesions the CNN raises 507 false alarms; the ResNet catches 96.9%
for 473. Every point where the two can be compared, the ResNet is ahead.

Accuracy went from 62.2% to 78.2% and false alarms fell from 282 to 159 at
the default rule, which says the same thing: the model is right more often
in both directions.

Per class the gains are large and even. bcc recall went 26.1% to 66.7%, bkl
precision 44.5% to 75.2%, nv recall 67.8% to 84.8%. df reached 100% recall,
all 21 of them, though on 21 images that is a fragile number and its 46.7%
precision shows it still over-calls the class.

Melanoma recall slipped slightly, 68.0% to 66.3%, and 41 melanomas were
called ordinary moles against the CNN's 10. That looks alarming until you
notice the CNN reached its number by calling almost everything suspicious;
it raised 282 false alarms to the ResNet's 159. At a matched operating point
the ResNet finds more melanomas.

Training loss fell to 0.2505 while validation loss bottomed at 0.6373 in
epoch 7 and drifted up afterwards. That gap is overfitting starting, and it
means 15 epochs is already more than this setup needs. Seven would have done.
