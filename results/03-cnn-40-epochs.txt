SkinLesionCNN, augmented, 40 epochs
===================================

epoch last, which meant it was being cut off rather than finishing.

What the numbers say
--------------------
Longer training improved everything except the number this project exists to
move. Validation loss is the best of the three by a clear margin, accuracy is
the highest, precision is the highest, and false alarms fell from 446 to 282.
Missed cancers went the wrong way, 30 up to 65.

The reason is that the network got better and, with that, more sure of
itself. It no longer guesses towards the malignant classes as freely, which
cuts false alarms and lets real cases slip through.

That is an argument against the decision rule, not against the longer run.
Taking whichever class scores highest is a poor way to decide something where
the two mistakes cost such different amounts. This model knows more than the
02 model does; it is simply being read at the wrong operating point.

df is the surprise: 14.3% recall in 02, 57.1% here, on 82 training examples.
Precision stayed at 9.6%, so it is over-calling df badly, and 60 ordinary
moles were labelled dermatofibroma. With 21 df images in validation, a swing
of a few images moves this number a lot. Not worth reading much into.

Training loss reached 1.1690 and was still falling, and the best epoch was 37
out of 40, so there may be a little more to gain. Validation loss bounced
between 0.95 and 1.23 in the last ten epochs, which points at the learning
rate being too large to settle rather than at a lack of epochs.
