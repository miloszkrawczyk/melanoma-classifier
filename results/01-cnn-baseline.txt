SkinLesionCNN, first run, no augmentation
=========================================

Written up so the README has real numbers to quote at the end of the project.
This is the baseline everything later gets compared against.

Setup
-----
model            SkinLesionCNN, 4 conv blocks, 391,175 parameters
input            224x224 RGB
normalisation    HAM10000 training statistics
                 mean (0.764, 0.546, 0.571), std (0.140, 0.152, 0.169)
loss             CrossEntropyLoss with class weights (inverse frequency)
optimiser        Adam, lr 0.001
batch size       32
epochs           15
device           mps
split            by lesion_id, seed 42, 70/15/15
augmentation     none
train/val/test   6986 / 1521 / 1508 images

What the numbers say
--------------------
The class weights did their job. nv has 93.6% precision against only 64.1%
recall, meaning the network is reluctant to answer "ordinary mole" but is
almost always right when it does. That reluctance is exactly what the 12:1
penalty was for, and it is also why accuracy sits below the naive baseline.

The level is still poor. 60.8% recall on malignant lesions means four cancers
in ten go unnoticed, alongside 250 false alarms.

The mel row is the one that matters. Of 178 melanomas the network found 82,
called 46 of them benign keratosis, and called 24 an ordinary mole. Those 24
are the dangerous errors: a patient hears "nothing to worry about". Melanoma
against benign keratosis is a genuinely hard pair, hard for dermatologists
too, so that confusion is at least the expected kind.

df came out at 23.8% recall and 7.6% precision on 82 training examples. No
weighting invents information that is not there.

vasc is the opposite shape: 87.5% recall against 26.9% precision. Vascular
lesions are red and easy to spot, and with a weight of 9.42 the network would
rather over-call them than miss one.

Training loss was still falling at epoch 15 and never came close to zero, so
this is underfitting rather than overfitting. Validation loss swung between
1.04 and 1.53 between neighbouring epochs, which points at the small
validation set and at rare classes carrying enormous weight in the average.
